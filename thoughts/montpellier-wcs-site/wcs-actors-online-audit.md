# Montpellier-area West Coast Swing actors — online audit

Research started: 2026-08-16  
Geographic scope: Montpellier plus Mauguio, Baillargues, Milhaud, Saint-Thibéry, Saint-Aunès, Saint-Gély-du-Fesc, Vendargues, Saint-Jean-de-Védas, and Fabrègues.

## How to read this list

This is a public-source discovery audit, not yet a definitive community directory. An actor is included when a public source connects it to West Coast Swing (WCS) in the geographic scope. In the tables, “current” means that an actor-controlled page has a live offer, a 2026–2027 schedule is published, or a live municipal listing still names the activity. “2025–2026 confirmed” means the ending season is evidenced but the 2026–2027 offer is not yet verified. Venue operators are listed separately from the associations or teachers using them because both may matter to a future community website.

The absence of a search result is not evidence that no activity exists. Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, and private groups are only partly indexed, so the “none confirmed” towns need direct local verification.

## Historical or ambiguous leads to verify

- ARPAC, Blue Pearl, Dancing Productions, Madswingers, Move Addict, and Virginie Tempo Danse appear in the 2016–2017 WCS Montpellier directory. They may be closed, renamed, inactive in WCS, or active mainly through social media.
- Dancing Productions was founded in Saint-Aunès by Alexandre Hulot according to a third-party teacher biography, but no current WCS offer was confirmed.
- Didier Sellam / Rock System in Fabrègues appears in older or third-party sources and remains connected to other local dance teaching, but a current local WCS schedule was not confirmed.
- The relationship among L’Atelier Danses, SHAG DANSE, and some shared contacts should be clarified to avoid duplicate directory entries.
- The relationship among Alma Dance, Step Up, El Salón de Tango, and Swing Cat is that of school/association and host venue in some cases; they should not be collapsed into one actor.

## Early findings for the website hypothesis

- The information is genuinely fragmented across actor websites, municipal directories, HelloAsso, Meetup, event aggregators, and social networks.
- A newcomer can find several schools, but comparing location, level, schedule, teacher, and whether an activity is a weekly class or a one-off stage requires opening many sources.
- “Montpellier” is often used as a marketing label for activities physically held in Mauguio, Saint-Jean-de-Védas, or Fabrègues. A future site needs both a marketed area and an exact municipality/address.
- Venue and organizer are frequently different actors. The data model needs explicit `organized_by`, `taught_by`, and `held_at` relationships.
- Freshness is the main trust risk. Search results mix 2017 archives, 2024 events, current 2025–2026 schedules, and early 2026–2027 announcements without consistently signaling which is live.
- There is already precedent for a neutral volunteer hub in WCS Montpellier, but its visible archive is stale. Before building, learn why it stopped and whether its owner or community wants it revived, replaced, or linked.
